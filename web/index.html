The discipline

When the data isn't there, DRISHTI says so.

Cosmos 2542 and 2543 shadowed a classified U.S. reconnaissance satellite, USA 245, through early 2020. DRISHTI reconstructs the two Russian actors' maneuvers in full — but the target itself has zero archived public elements.

So its position is withheld, not estimated. A system that invents the one number it can't measure can't be trusted with the thousands it can.
